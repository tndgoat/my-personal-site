Crisp morning air and a biting headwind rolling off the water, but cresting the coastal ridge right as the horizon ignited in amber made every cold mile worth it.

**Highlights of today's ride:**
- The headwind on the outbound stretch kept the tempo honest, but holding 18+ mph felt surprisingly smooth.
- Watched the golden hour spill over the calm waters of the bay — one of those moments that resets the entire week.
- Quick pitstop at mile 18 for an espresso before turning with the tailwind back home.

Legs felt surprisingly fresh all the way to the door. More mornings like this, please.
